WEEK 16 — regional XCO2 context map (descriptive, not a model; closes the
                        blueprint's mapping-track item)

- Question: closes a named gap from the project blueprint's mapping
  track (same descriptive spirit as the regional-XCO2-context literature
  surveyed there, e.g. STEL, DNN-CAMS): what does background CO2 look
  like across the study region, using data already collected rather than
  a new download? Explicitly not a prediction model -- no fitting, no
  per-plant emission estimate, just a spatial coverage-and-value picture
  for the paper's Methods/Data section.

- regional_xco2_map.py (new): pools every OCO-3 sounding already
  downloaded across all 30 plants (data/*_soundings.npz, no new Earth
  Engine call) into one dataset -- 52,898 soundings total, verified by
  direct sum across all 30 files. Bins into a 0.5-degree grid (per
  explicit spec, not tuned) over a bounding box built from the 30
  plants' own lat/lon extent (14.70-29.92 N, 69.55-88.10 E) plus a
  1.0-degree margin, rounded outward to the nearest 0.5-degree line:
  13.5-31.0 N, 68.5-89.5 E (35 x 42 = 1,470 possible cells).

- Per-cell statistic: mean XCO2 (ppm), reported only for cells with at
  least 3 soundings (MIN_SOUNDINGS_PER_CELL=3, per explicit direction --
  1-2 soundings isn't enough to trust a per-cell mean). Cells below that
  floor are masked as NaN, identically to empty cells -- "no data" must
  not read as "low CO2." The floor itself is recorded directly in the
  output JSON's n_soundings_min_required field, not left as a silent
  default.

- Result: 130 of 1,470 cells (8.8%) have enough coverage to report a
  value. 9 cells masked as too-sparse (1-2 soundings), 1,331 cells
  entirely empty. The real finding here is coverage sparsity itself, not
  any particular XCO2 value: most of the study region has no usable
  OCO-3 data at this resolution, and what coverage exists is
  concentrated almost entirely around the 30 plants' own near-plant/
  background sampling zones -- the same sparsity story §5.2.1's
  per-facility sounding counts already told (1 to over 7,000 per plant),
  now shown to be a spatial property of the dataset too, not just a
  per-facility one. Where cells do have enough coverage, mean XCO2 ranges
  roughly 410-419 ppm regionally, consistent with ordinary background
  dry-air XCO2 variation rather than any per-cell anomaly.

- Output: data/regional_xco2_map.png (heatmap, masked NaNs, with the 30
  plant locations overlaid, sized and colored by CEA absolute emissions
  for context) and data/regional_xco2_grid.json (metadata plus the 130
  non-masked cells: lat_bin_center, lon_bin_center, mean_xco2_ppm,
  n_soundings each).

- VERDICT: descriptive success, not a research finding to argue over --
  the map does what it was asked to do (show background CO2 context) and
  honestly shows how little of the region is actually observed, which is
  itself useful context for reading every Track B number in this paper.
