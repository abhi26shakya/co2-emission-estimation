WEEK 11 — DONE (facility-level train/test split for Track A, fixing a
                 leakage risk flagged since Week 7)

- RESEARCH_PLAN.md flagged since Week 7 that Track A's classifier
  (train_3channel.py) used a random tile-level train/test split, meaning
  the same physical plant's monthly tiles could land in both train and
  test -- a leakage risk that inflates reported accuracy. This week fixes
  it and, per the project's convention of measuring deltas rather than
  just swapping in a fix silently, runs BOTH the old (leaky) and new
  (facility-level) split side by side for a direct before/after
  comparison.

- train_3channel.py changes:
  * load_folder() now returns a facility/site group id per tile (filename
    minus the trailing _YYYY_MM.npy suffix), e.g. "VINDH_CHAL_STPS" or
    "city_Delhi".
  * facility_level_split(): splits by group, not by tile, so a site's
    tiles never appear on both sides. Stratified by class -- with only 5
    positive-class facilities, a plain group shuffle risked a degenerate
    test set with zero positive examples (making recall undefined), so
    positive and negative groups are held out separately, guaranteeing
    both classes appear in test.
  * tile_level_split(): the original random-split logic, kept (not
    removed) specifically to reproduce the old number for comparison.
  * Facility-split checkpoints are saved under new filenames
    (detector3_{tag}_facility_split.pt) rather than overwriting the
    existing detector3_2ch_hard_only.pt / detector3_2ch_mixed.pt --
    extract_activity_signal.py already depends on the latter (used to
    build the already-committed data/activity_signals.json), and silently
    changing what that checkpoint contains would invalidate a committed
    result without a corresponding re-extraction. See NEXT.

- Result: the leakage was real and substantial, and the "mixed" model in
  particular is far weaker than its reported accuracy suggested.

  hard_only  tile-level (leaky, reproduces the historical number exactly):
             81.2% accuracy, 68% plant recall, 92% negative recall
             facility-level (fixed):
             67.3% accuracy, 54% plant recall, 80% negative recall
             -- a genuine ~14-point drop, not noise.

  mixed      tile-level (leaky):
             86.8% accuracy, 53% plant recall, 96% negative recall
             facility-level (fixed):
             83.3% accuracy, 8% plant recall, 98% negative recall
             -- overall accuracy barely moves, but plant recall collapses
             from 53% to 8%. The model is essentially guessing "not a
             plant" for a genuinely unseen facility and getting away with
             it because negatives vastly outnumber positive-class
             facilities in the tile-count-weighted accuracy metric (25
             negative sites vs. 5 positive). This is exactly the kind of
             leakage-driven overconfidence RESEARCH_PLAN.md's Sec 3 warned
             about, and it's a much bigger effect for "mixed" than for
             "hard_only".

- Consequence for Week 10's activity-signal work: detector3_2ch_mixed.pt
  -- the exact checkpoint extract_activity_signal.py used to build
  data/activity_signals.json -- was trained on tiles from the SAME 4
  facilities already used as Track B's original positive-class examples
  (Vindhyachal, Sasan, Mundra, Tirora). Only the 5 new Week 9 facilities
  (Talcher, Rihand, Sipat, ChandrapurCoal, Anpara) were genuinely unseen
  to it. Given this week's 8% held-out-facility recall for that exact
  checkpoint, the activity signal computed for those 5 facilities should
  be treated with real skepticism -- the "activity signal broadly tracks
  Q" finding from Week 10 may reflect the model recognizing 4 memorized
  sites rather than detecting genuine combustion activity at novel
  locations. This doesn't invalidate the physics-based Q estimates
  (Track B) at all, only the CNN-derived activity-signal cross-check
  layered on top of them.
